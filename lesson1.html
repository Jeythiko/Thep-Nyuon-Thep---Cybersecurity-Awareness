Hackers & Scammers

This section will clue you up on how to identify the hacker and scammer, learn more about this lesson.

Who is hacker?

A hacker is an individual who attempts to gain unauthorized access to an organization's, company's, or personal information. Hackers may use different techniques to access your accounts, devices, or private information without your knowledge or permission.

A hacker may contact you through messages, emails, fake websites, or other online methods in an attempt to steal your personal information. Once they gain access to your information, they may use it for malicious purposes, such as stealing your identity, accessing your accounts, or demanding money in exchange for restoring access to your data.

Hackers are often described as internet predators because they may hide their identities and use sophisticated techniques to target individuals and organizations. In this website, you will learn practical ways to protect your personal information and reduce the risk of being hacked.

Who Is a Scammer?

A scammer is someone who deceives people in order to steal their money, personal information, or other valuable things. Scammers use lies, tricks, and manipulative tactics to gain a victim's trust and then take advantage of them.

Common Types of Scammers Online scammers

Use fake links,fake investment opportunities, hacked accounts, romance scams, fake websites, and other online methods to trick people into giving away their personal information or money.

Phone scammers

Pretend to be representatives of banks, police departments, companies, or other trusted organizations.

Email scammers

Send phishing emails asking you to "verify" your account, reset your password, or provide sensitive information.

Social media scammers

Create fake pages, fake giveaways, or impersonate other people to trick users into giving away money or personal information.
