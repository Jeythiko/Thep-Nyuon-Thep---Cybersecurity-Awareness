Stay Protected

Simple habits can make a big difference.

Follow these basic security practices whenever you use the internet.

01

Don't click suspicious links

Verify unexpected links before opening them, especially when they come from unknown people.

02

Use strong passwords

Use unique passwords for important accounts and avoid sharing them.

03

Enable 2FA

Two-factor authentication provides an additional layer of protection for accounts.

04

Protect verification codes

Never give login or verification codes to someone who contacts you unexpectedly.

05

Keep devices updated

Install trusted operating-system and application updates when they are available.

06

Question unrealistic offers

Be careful with offers promising easy money, prizes, jobs or guaranteed profits.
